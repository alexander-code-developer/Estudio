REGRESAR AL PRINCIPAL

🔧 Causative Form: HAVE / GET · (have, has, had, will have, get, gets, got, will get)

Expresamos que alguien hace que otra persona realice una acción por nosotros.

📌 HAVE + objeto + participio
I have my car repaired.
She has her hair cut.
They had the house painted.
We will have the documents signed.
⚡ GET + objeto + participio
I get my car repaired.
She gets her nails done.
He got his phone fixed.
They will get the report finished.
🎯 Estructura clave:
Sujeto + have/get (conjugado) + objeto + participio pasado
Present: have/has + object + past participle
Past: had/got + object + past participle
Future: will have/will get + object + past participle

💡 Diferencia: "have" es más formal, "get" es más informal/cotidiano. Ambos funcionan igual. ¡Practica con más de 200 ejercicios!
