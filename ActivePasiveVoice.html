🎤➡️📢 VOZ PASIVA · Presente Simple (is/are)

Transforma oraciones activas a pasivas. Ahora con normalización inteligente: acepta isn't o is not, aren't o are not, sin importar mayúsculas, espacios o puntuación final.

📌 Estructura: Sujeto + is/are (o negación) + participio + (by + agente)
✅ The meal is prepared by the chef.
❌ The meal isn't prepared / is not prepared.
❓ Is the meal prepared by the chef?
Activa: My mother doesn't bake a cake. ➡ Pasiva válida: "A cake isn't baked by my mother." o "A cake is not baked by my mother."
